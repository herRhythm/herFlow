Why This Card Exists

You Don’t Have to Get It Right. You Just Have to Show Up.

Most men never got a guide like this, not because nobody cared, because nobody wrote one. That silence gets passed down, father to son, one generation not knowing what to say, teaching the next one the same.

We looked for research on men supporting daughters through this exact moment, in Kenya, in Africa, anywhere close. We found plenty on fathers and maternal health, almost nothing on fathers and periods. That’s not because it doesn’t matter. It’s because almost nobody has asked yet.

She’s not waiting for the right words. She’s waiting to see if you stay in the room.
