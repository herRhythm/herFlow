Coming of Age · First Period Kit

Menarche

Menarche is the first menstrual period — signaling the start of reproductive capacity. It is a major milestone in female development, and we at HerRhythm would like to be part of that experience.

A thoughtfully designed first-period companion that helps girls understand what is happening to their bodies, manage common symptoms, and feel supported by the people around them.

We combine education, comfort, and practical menstrual care into one beautiful experience — because every girl deserves to begin her rhythm with confidence, joy, and unshakeable self-belief.
