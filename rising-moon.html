What's inside

The 5 pillars

Learn, Care, Comfort, Support, Impact — every Rising Moon kit stands on all five.

📖

Learn

Understand your body. Own your rhythm.

When you understand your menstrual cycle, you make better decisions for your health and wellbeing. Every Rising Moon Experience includes trusted, easy-to-understand resources developed with healthcare professionals and menstrual educators.

  • Understanding your menstrual cycle
  • Managing common period symptoms
  • Nutrition and self-care during your period
  • Tracking your cycle
  • Menstrual hygiene and product choices
  • When to seek medical advice

Knowledge turns uncertainty into confidence.

🌙

Care

Stay ready. Stay a step ahead.

Your cycle shouldn't catch you by surprise. The Care experience helps you stay one step ahead, so you can focus on school, work, sports, or life — not your period.

  • Personalized cycle prep card
  • Panty liners for lighter flow days
  • Pocket tissues
  • Wet wipes for freshening up on the go
  • A reusable DigniBag to keep your essentials organized wherever you are

Because confidence begins with being prepared.

💜

Comfort

Be kind to your body.

Periods can bring cramps, fatigue, bloating, and mood changes. We've thoughtfully selected comforting essentials to help you slow down, recharge, and feel supported throughout your cycle.

  • Deep Heat period patch
  • Herbal tea selection (ginger, peppermint, or chamomile)
  • Dark chocolate for those days
  • Hot water bottle

Small comforts can make a big difference.

🤝

Support

You're never navigating your period alone.

When you choose Rising Moon, you become part of a growing community of girls and women who believe menstruation should be met with confidence — not stigma.

  • Menstrual health tips and educational content
  • Self-care reminders throughout your cycle
  • Access to workshops and community events
  • Access to our health professionals
  • A safe space to learn, ask questions, and grow
  • Stories from women who inspire and uplift one another

Together, we're creating a future where every period is met with dignity.

🌍

Impact

Your period creates possibilities for another girl.

Every Rising Moon Kit purchased funds 3 Dignikits for a girl who needs it. Each Dignikit contains 2 packs of sanitary towels, menstrual education, bathroom tissues, wet wipes, and delivery to her.

  • Deliver a full Dignikit to an underserved girl
  • Support menstrual health education in schools
  • Break menstrual stigma through awareness programs
  • Build a generation of confident, informed young women
  • Make men our allies instead of bystanders

Together, we'll help her know her rhythm, so she can flow through life empowered.
